The testbed: a simplified Montreal¶

Experiments are run on one network, in the way much of transport research uses Sioux Falls (24 nodes): small enough to be read off a map, fixed, and the same for everybody. The testbed is the scenario montreal.

The network¶

25 nodes and 51 two-way roads, about 21 km by 17 km (agmsim/montreal.py, network: {type: montreal}). It keeps what shapes traffic in the city:

  • an island reached over a handful of crossings: the Champlain, Victoria and Jacques-Cartier bridges and the Lafontaine tunnel to the South Shore, four bridges to Laval;

  • a centre on its south side: Downtown, McGill, Old Montreal, the Quartier Latin;

  • a ring of expressways: the A-40 Metropolitan, the A-15 Decarie, the A-20, the Ville-Marie and the A-25;

  • the arterials between them: Sherbrooke, Saint-Denis, Papineau, Pie-IX, Henri-Bourassa and others.

Positions are approximate (read off a map by district), lengths are straight-line distances times 1.2, speeds and lanes are typical values for the class of road. It is an abstraction to experiment on, not a calibrated model of the city.

The morning¶

agmsim run montreal simulates a weekday from 06:00 to 12:00 with everything the simulator has:

Part

In the testbed

Travellers

900, living where people live (weights by district), 65% with a car; most set out around 08:00

Places

21 named destinations: downtown offices, the Technoparc, YUL airport, McGill, UdeM, UQAM, three hospitals, markets and malls, the Bell Centre, the Olympic Park

Private cars

one per car-owning traveller, electric, level L1 (re-route on traffic)

On-demand cars

a fleet of 40, level L1

Transit

four lines on their own right of way, drawn as loops: Orange, Green, South, West

Traffic lights

at 10 junctions of the inner city, deciding by their own queues (L1)

Charging stations

7 stations, 17 chargers, dynamic price (L1)

Orchestrator

publishes advisories (L1); a cordon around Downtown that can carry a charge

Event

a crash on the Decarie expressway, 07:30 to 08:10, traffic at 30% of its speed

With these settings and seed 1 every trip is completed, the mean trip takes 15 minutes, 63% of trips are made by car, 19% by transit and 18% by on-demand car; a run with the built-in decision models takes about 15 seconds.

Changing a condition¶

An experiment changes settings of the testbed and leaves everything else as it is:

agmsim run montreal --set agents.vehicle.agency=L0          # cars that follow their first route
agmsim run montreal --set agents.operator.initial_toll=2    # a charge of 2 USD to enter the centre
agmsim run montreal --set events=[]                         # the same morning without the crash
agmsim run montreal --set agents.traveler.count=1500        # more people

Every setting is described in Configuration.
