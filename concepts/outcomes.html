Outcome variables¶

Every run reports four system-level measures under outcomes. They lead the results on the page and in agmsim run.

Variable

Key

Meaning

Average waiting time

wait_time_s_mean

time travellers wait for transport (at a stop, or for a car they called), per completed trip; trips without a wait count as 0

VMT

vmt_miles (vkt_km)

vehicle miles travelled by private cars, on-demand cars and transit vehicles

VHT

vht_hours

vehicle hours travelled: time vehicles spend on the road

Average traffic speed

traffic_speed_mph (traffic_speed_kmh)

VMT ÷ VHT

Further measures follow them, by kind of agent:

Group

Examples

travelers

trips, completion rate, trip time (mean, 90th percentile), mode shares, generalised cost

vehicles

re-routes, tolls paid, proposals to owners, charging stops and queueing, empty kilometres

ondemand

requests served and turned down, empty kilometres, profit

bus

boardings, holds, stops skipped

evcs

sessions, revenue, utilisation, how evenly stations are used

signals

waiting at lights, switches

operator

advisories, charge, revenue

decisions

per kind: decision points, how often authority was used

llm

calls, tokens, fallbacks, latency, estimated cost

In Python: result.metrics["outcomes"]["traffic_speed_mph"]; on the command line agmsim run montreal --json.
