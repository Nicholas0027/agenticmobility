Every kind of agent is decided by one model: a built-in one, or a language model (LLM). Agency sets what it is allowed to decide. What each built-in model does is in the Guide.
A language model answers in about a second, and the run waits for it at every decision: as the page opens the testbed takes about half an hour (some 1,900 questions), with every kind on a language model several hours, with built-in models only a few seconds.

What they know memory, observation, interaction

KindRecalls its last
Agency says what a kind may decide; this says what it has to go on. Every decision model of a kind is handed the same. Kinds that find their own way through traffic also have a sight, a memory for slow roads, and other cars to hear from.
